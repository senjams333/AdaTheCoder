What we collect

When a parent or guardian creates an account we store their name, email address, city and country, and a scrambled (hashed) version of their password. Parents can choose to add their child's first name and age. We also record when the app is downloaded.

What we don't collect

Children don't create accounts. The app doesn't send your child's code, answers or progress to us; that stays on your computer. The app only asks our website for new lessons and app updates.

How we use it

We use your details to let you log in and download the app. We don't sell your details or show adverts.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from your account page. That removes your details from our records.
